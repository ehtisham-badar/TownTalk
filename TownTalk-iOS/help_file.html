A Guide to town talk

Signing up and getting started

Welcome to Town Talk! Before you start to chat with others, there are a few steps you need to take.

Town Talk App for Android or iOS

Download the Town Talk app for Android or iOS

Tap Create Account

Enter your phone number and verify

If prompted, enter your email and verify

Connect an account - connect your Apple, Google, or Facebook account for a streamlined login experience

Accept the terms of service, privacy policy, and community guidelines.

Set up your profile

Allow Town Talk access to all required permissions for a complete experience

How do I edit my profile?

To edit your profile info, tap the profile icon, then tap Edit profile.

Here, you can update your photo, name, usernyame, email, your city, and the radius to which you would like to see posts, We recommend updating your city every time you travel. We’re working on a way for “your city” to auto-update upon location change.

What can you do in Town Talk?

Town Talk offers a robust library of features - we’ll go through them below, please note we’ll give just an overview. If you’re interested in learning more about these features go navigate the app to get the full experience of what town talk has to offer; if not, contact us for a detailed description of our services. We’ll provide our explanations by screen.

Our home screen provides you with a feed within your chosen radius where everyone that uses town talk can post, if they are within your radius you’ll see their posts. You have the ability to see other towns’ posts and activities just in case you feel like being nosy. Your ability to change locations is located on top of your screen, notifications and search bar is also in your home screen.

What is the purpose of the search bar? The search bar allows you to search for other users, as well as necessary businesses nearby that you may find useful. Some of these businesses include restaurants, gas stations, gyms, malls, and much more.

Our explore page is one of our primary focus areas for Town Talk. The explore page gives you and other users the ability to locate nearby “hotspots.” Hotspots are locations, businesses, and the like where users of Town Talk are “checking in” to. When a user checks into a business, location, and the like - this speaks to Town Talk to let other users know that this location is worth checking into, but if it does turn out that this location is not worth checking into, scroll down to the business page and see “what people are saying” and see reviews, reviews from our own users - not Google.

Also included in our explore page, you’ll find #TrendingTowns - this gives you visibility into which towns nearby have the most activity in the past 24 hours. So don’t forget to invite your friends to the app and make your town rank 1 year around. (Incentives coming soon)

Next is our composing screen, this is where all the magic happens. In our composing screen aka posting screen, you have the ability to do multiple things. Don’t forget to look at our privacy policy, community guidelines, and terms of service to know what kind of posts are allowed and what is not allowed. Don’t forget this is your community, if you wouldn’t share it with them to their face, don’t post it to Town Talk, make sense?

You can post text, up to 300 characters

You can post your favorite videos & photos for others to see

And one of our most important features is the ability to tag businesses on a post. This ability is one we’re proud of because in the future we want to expand & partner with local & small businesses to provide deals, customer service, and announcements to users on their feeds. What purpose will this serve? Small businesses are the reasons our society grows and most people like you and I have jobs. Let’s give them a place where locals and small businesses can grow together on a personal level. So, go ahead and tag your favorite businesses or if you had a bad experience at your local Dunkin Donuts or Starbucks, tag them and let them know what happened and how they can do better, do not underestimate your voice.

Our message screen allows you to have private conversations with nearby users or neighbors without compromising any personal information that can be attached to you. Before we move on, we’d like to lay a few rules (these can also be found in our terms of service, privacy policy, and community guidelines) We ask users to not share personal information with anyone they do not know or to keep it simple, do not share sensitive information with anyone on Town Talk. Practice safety by blocking and reporting users who are breaking TOS, not following the general rule of thumb, and leave the rest in our hands. Do not share your location with users you do not know, no matter the circumstance, remember you can always report and block these users to keep them from sending you unsolicited messages. Do not share adult photos or videos with other users and most importantly, if these get shared with you, remember to report and block this user.

Our message screen allows you to message other users, send locations for a certain amount of time.. More features to come soon, believe in us.

You will have the ability to create group chats, add, remove, and have fun with the most interesting and funny users nearby. Make Town Talk your new extended community tool.

Don’t forget to check your “requests” to see if you have any new messages and discover new people and friends.

Finally, the profile page - you know how you don’t like going outside and looking bad? You don’t want to get caught outside looking a mess, but even if that happens we have our days.

Make your profile how you would like to represent yourself to your community. We have more customization updates coming for the profile page because we believe our Town Talk users’ profiles are their looks when they go outside really quick to get some food. In your profile, you’ll see how to edit your profile and settings page.

Settings are located in the upper right corner of your profile page. Our settings page contains a lot of useful information. These useful information tabs you see are all relevant to keep you, your profile, and your community safe outside and within Town Talk. Below are the tabs you will find within our settings page and purposes

“Push Notifications” this will notify when something involving you or someone has been tagged, and other types of notifications. This will let you manage your notifications such as turning on message notifications, turning off tagged notification posts or replies to your posts

“Blocked Users” will allow you to manage users you have blocked for having bad vibes, and leave them in the blocked list for one more day, maybe they’ll change. If not, keep them in there, and don’t let them ruin your Town Talk and community experience.

“Change Password” gives you the ability to change your password, we recommend changing your password every 90 days to keep you, your profile, and your community safe from hackers who have nothing better to do.

“Privacy policy” to keep it simple, this document is a legal document that tells you how Town Talk plans to gather information, how we plan on using this info, discloses, and how we manage your data here at Town Talk. We recommend you thoroughly read through this document for a more detailed explanation.

“Terms of Service” is another legal document that is an agreement between Town Talk (us) and you (user). We’re providing you with this service therefore you must abide by our terms. We recommend you read through our terms of service document fully to get a more detailed explanation.

“Community Guidelines”

“EULA” AKA End-User license agreement, Is another legal (last one i promise) that defines your right and restrictions for using Town Talk. We recommend you read thoroughly through our EULA agreement to get a more detailed explanation.

“Contact Us” We’re here for you and your community, contact us to give us feedback and feature ideas and tweaks and most importantly to answer your questions, courtesy of our support team (support@towntalkapp.com). Contact us if you have any legal questions (legal@towntalkapp.com). Finally, contact us if you would like to become one of our partners (business@towntalkapp.com)

Can I delete my Town Talk profile?

You can 100% delete your town talk account, we understand sometimes social media can be a bit overwhelming and everyone deserves a break here and there. You can delete your account from your settings page, do note once you delete your account - you will lose everything from posts, likes and dislikes, groups, reviews, and check-ins. But do not worry, we’ll always be here once you decide to come back.

Yes, we do offer dark mode, we at Town Talk love it too so go ahead and turn it on.
